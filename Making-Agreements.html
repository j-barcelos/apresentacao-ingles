Key language

Useful Phrases for the Deal

Four moves decide every negotiation: ask, answer, accept and refuse. These phrases keep the conversation friendly and professional.

01 · Ask
"What exactly does the service include?"
"How much does it cost per month?"
02 · Answer
"That's a great question."
"Let me check that for you."
03 · Say Yes
"Yes, we can do that."
"That works for us."
04 · Say No
"I'm afraid that's not possible, but we can offer…"

The Negotiation

Nova Office needs IT support for its new office. It4U wants to close the deal. Listen and read: how do they ask, answer, say no — and still agree?

Nova Office — Client
"How much does the basic plan cost? And what exactly does it include?"
It4U — Provider
"It's €80 a month. That includes helpdesk support and a four-hour response time."
Nova Office — Client
"I'm afraid that's over our budget. Could you offer a discount for a 12-month contract?"
It4U — Provider
"Let me check… Yes, we can do that. Shall we confirm the details?"

Language focus

Say No — Politely

Softeners keep the conversation friendly — even when the answer is no.
✗ Too direct
"No. That's too expensive."  ·  "We can't do that."
✓ Polite
"I'm afraid that's a little over our budget."  ·  "I'm sorry, we can't do that — but we can offer…"

Role-play: Make the Deal

Step 1 · Prepare & Open

Student A is the client; Student B is the IT provider. Read your role cards, decide the services you need and set a budget — then open the negotiation with clear questions.

Step 2 · Negotiate & Close

Answer politely, say no without losing the deal, then confirm the details, agree a final price — and sign your agreement!
